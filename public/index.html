Synthetic staffing journal

Every reported nursing minute, checked against the rows that support it.

ShiftLedger reconciles a synthetic day of reported staffing minutes against payroll and agency evidence, then shows each finding with the exact rows it cites. It runs entirely in your browser on fictional data and fictional rules.

Open synthetic review What it is not

What one review covers

  1. Three totals, never blended

    Raw reported, deduplicated reported and eligible support, in integer minutes with hours alongside.

  2. Findings with their rows

    Filter by employee, date, role or finding, then open the reported and support rows each finding cites.

  3. Notes that change nothing

    Reviewer decisions annotate a run. They never alter totals, and each run stays in an immutable history.

  4. Exports you can check

    Download the report as JSON or formula-safe CSV, then rerun a corrected source and compare.

Boundaries

  • Synthetic data and explicitly fictional local rules only.
  • Not a CMS or PBJ validator, not certified compliance, not an assessment of care.
  • “Unsupported” means unsupported by the supplied evidence, not fraud or proof that work did not happen.
  • No accounts, server, storage or multi-tenant service. Imported files are read locally in the tab.
  • SHA-256 values identify content. They are not signatures.
